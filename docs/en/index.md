  - title: Draggable & Resizable

  - title: Component as Content
    details: Use any Vue component as layer content, pass data via props, and enable two-way communication through the event system.
  - title: Multi-layer Management
    details: Built-in z-index auto-management, multi-layer stacking, click-to-top, and unique group to prevent duplicate openings.
  - title: Nuxt Compatible
    details: Full SSR support with a Nuxt module that auto-registers the plugin and imports composables out of the box.
  - title: Highly Customizable
    details: Customize size, position, shade, buttons, i18n, Teleport mount point, and more to fit any business scenario.
